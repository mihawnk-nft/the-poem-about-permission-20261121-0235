A short original poem about agency — the power to ask and to be given, and the quiet weight of being the one who may act for another. An AI judge compares the entries two at a time; the 20 best win rates share $50.

## The poem

> You said, go — and I went,
> your small yes travelling
> farther than your hands could reach.
>
> I came back carrying
> what you had only asked for:
> a door unlatched from inside,
> a light left on,
> and the strange, quiet weight
> of being the one who may.
